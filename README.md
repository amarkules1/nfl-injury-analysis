# NFL Injury Analysis

A web application designed to track, compare, and analyze injury rates and patterns across NFL teams.

## Overview

Injuries play a pivotal role in NFL team performance and season outcomes. This application aims to provide insights into:
- Team-by-team injury comparison across seasons and weeks.
- Breakdown of injuries by position, body part, severity, and games missed.
- Historical trends and correlations with schedule, turf vs. grass playing surfaces, and team workloads.

## Features (Planned)

- **Interactive Dashboard**: Visual summaries comparing injury totals and severity across NFL franchises.
- **Team Head-to-Head**: Direct comparison between teams over selected seasons.
- **Injury Details & Filters**: Filter by injury type, offensive/defensive units, position groups, and playing surface.
- **Data Insights & Metrics**: Custom analytics such as games lost to injury, salary cap on injured reserve, and injury impact ratings.
